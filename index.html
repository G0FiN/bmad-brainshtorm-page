MonoTrue

A mobile finance app that tracks what you actually spent — not what the bank recorded.

I paid 1 000 ₴ for a group lunch. Monobank shows it as food expense. Colleagues returned 800 ₴. Monobank shows it as income. Both are lies. This app lets you record the truth: you spent 200.

Technique 1 · Assumption Reversal

Every assumption flipped.

Category mapping is done transaction by transaction
MCC codes seed the first suggestion. Every override trains the engine. ATB can be Food or Car — the app learns.
suggestion engine
Monobank is the only account
Two account types: Bank (auto-synced) + Cash (manual). Total = bank + cash. ATM withdrawal = transfer between them.
account model
In-app total = cash + bank sum
App Total = all tracked income − tracked expenses. Actual Total = bank + cash. Gap = Actual − App. User manages the gap.
gap concept
Bank sync failures mean manual entry
Manual entry is first-class always — not a fallback. Both bank and cash transactions matter equally.
manual entry
Ignored transactions are gone forever
Raw Monobank transactions are always stored and accessible. Ignored = excluded from analytics, not deleted.
data model
The phone is the app
Phone is a thin client only. All logic and data live on the existing .NET backend + MongoDB + Redis.
architecture

Technique 2 · SCAMPER

Seven lenses on the reference app.

S
Substitute
Swap the heavy preset category library for minimal defaults + categories that grow from actual use. What you haven't used doesn't clutter the screen.
C
Combine
Merge the triage inbox into the home screen as a collapsible strip below the balances. Gap number and inbox are visually adjacent — one motivates the other.
A
Adapt
Borrow the banking app idiom: transaction detail shows raw Monobank data (merchant, MCC, timestamp) alongside the user's interpretation (category, corrected amount). Source and truth, side by side.
M
Modify
The donut chart gets a second ring. Outer = tracked spending by category. Inner = the gap. The gap visually shrinks as you clear the inbox — a live progress ring.
P
Put to other use
Manual entry form goes tap-only — only the amount is typed. Category, date, account: all tapped from pickers. Speed and consistency over flexibility.
E
Eliminate
Cut for MVP: recurring payments, reminders, photo attachments, tags, long comments, share/rate/support, in-app purchases. Ship lean, earn trust.
R
Reverse
Instead of user visiting app to check finances: the app delivers a weekly digest — spending summary, top category, unprocessed count — as a push notification. Finance finds you.

Technique 3 · Starbursting

Six questions that shaped the app.

Mobile Money
Tracking App
+ Monobank
WHO
Not just personal — a multi-user product. ~5 beta testers, ~100 users month one. Each user has fully isolated data and their own Monobank token.
WHAT
The aha moment: connect Monobank → see real balance + transactions in inbox. Charts are earned through processing — not faked on day one.
WHERE
Monobank connect happens during registration — already in the existing web app flow. The mobile app reuses the same backend.
WHEN
The app breaks when the inbox backlogs and notifications become noise. The swipe model is the direct defense — two interactions for the happy path.
WHY
Monobank tracks bank activity. This app tracks financial truth. A 1 000 ₴ group lunch where colleagues return 800 ₴ should show as 200 ₴ food expense.
HOW
Full CRUD on every transaction. Nothing is locked after processing. Go back, fix the amount, change the category — any time.

Scope

What ships. What waits.

Must Have
  • Two-layer data model (raw + curated)
  • Home screen: balances + gap + inbox strip
  • Swipe-based inbox (track / ignore / edit)
  • Full CRUD on transactions
  • Category system with CRUD
  • Onboarding + analytics start date
  • Bank + cash account types
  • Transaction detail (raw vs user view)
  • Basic settings + PIN lock
Should Have
  • Category suggestion engine (MCC + history)
  • Two-ring donut chart
  • Smart inbox-nudge notifications
  • ATM withdrawal → transfer detection
  • Full analytics screen (bar charts, time filters)
Could Have
  • Weekly digest push notification
  • Multiple Monobank jars / multi-card
  • Data import (migration from reference app)
Won't Have (MVP)
  • Recurring payments
  • Reminders
  • Photo attachments
  • Tags
  • Share / rate / support links
  • In-app purchases
  • Auto-categorization on first sync

Synthesis

The connections that matter.

1
The inbox IS the truth-making machine — not a chore, it's the product. The value proposition (financial truth vs bank activity) and the swipe inbox are the same idea expressed two ways. Every swipe is a truth decision: "did I really spend 1 000 ₴, or was it 200?" The inbox is the reason the charts mean something.
2
The failure mode and the swipe UX are mirror images. Inbox backlog + notification fatigue = the app becomes useless. Swipe right with pre-filled suggestion + one tap to confirm = the direct defense. One is the problem, one is the fix.
3
The two-ring donut and the gap number are the same idea in two forms. The gap on the home screen is a number. The inner ring on the donut is that gap, made visual. Together they make the unprocessed state impossible to ignore — everywhere, without being annoying.
4
Analytics start date and data import are two sides of one migration story. "How do I get my history in?" — bank history via analytics start date, manual history via import. Two mechanisms, one user problem.
5
The whole app respects user agency over automation. No auto-categorization. Smart suggestions, not rules. Batched notifications, not per-transaction. Editable everything. A consistent philosophy: the app helps you understand your money — it doesn't categorize it for you.
Value Proposition

Monobank tracks what the bank recorded.
This app tracks what actually happened.
